# Page Plan — Anomalies

- Page ID: `anomalies`
- Priority: P0
- Pilot: no
- Search intent: help players recognize anomaly evidence without pretending every anomaly has one permanent action.
- Primary answer: classify the evidence surface, identify the exact named finding, and apply the current bulletin's hierarchy.
- Required sections: official boundary, category table, documents and behavior, external and photo, temperature/organs/bones, UV, blood, elevator, decision tree, unknowns, FAQ.
- Evidence boundary: official category signals plus dated launch-build tool observations.
- Internal links: checkpoint guide, medical tests, quarantine.
- Exclusions: exhaustive probability claims, invented anomaly names, universal action table, ending thresholds.
- Success condition: a player can choose the correct tool and avoid the assumption that every abnormal result is hostile.
